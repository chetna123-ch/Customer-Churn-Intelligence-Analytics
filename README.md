# 📊 Customer Churn & Revenue Impact Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes customer, subscription, and customer-support data to understand **who is churning, why customers leave, when churn occurs, and how much revenue is at risk**.

The project simulates a real-world OTT/subscription business environment and demonstrates how SQL and Python can be combined to transform raw relational data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based businesses.

The objective of this project is to answer:

* Which customers are most likely to churn?
* Which subscription plans have the highest churn?
* Is monthly or annual subscription more likely to churn?
* Which geographic regions have higher churn?
* Does customer support activity relate to churn?
* How much recurring revenue is at risk?
* Which customer segments should the business prioritize for retention?

## 📈 Key KPIs

The project calculates:

### Churn Rate

```text
Churned Customers / Total Customers
```

### Retention Rate

```text
1 - Churn Rate
```

### ARPU

```text
Total Monthly Charges / Active Customers
```

### Average Customer Tenure

Average duration between subscription start and cancellation/current date.

### Revenue at Risk

Monthly revenue generated by customers with high churn risk.

### Escalation Rate

Percentage of complaints that resulted in escalation.

### Average Complaints per Customer

Total complaints divided by unique customers.
